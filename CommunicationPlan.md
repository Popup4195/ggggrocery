# Communication Plan

### Daily — Quick Synchronize

**Duration:** anytime

**Platform:** Discord

**Purpose:** Quick status updates, blockers or clarifications.

**Format:**

- Each member posts what they worked on yesterday, what they plan to do today, and any issues



### Weekly — Team Synchronize

**Duration:** 1 hour~

**Platform:** CO235, face to face

**Purpose:** Review progress, milestone tracking and next steps.

**Format:**

- Review timeline and milestones
- Discuss risks, dependencies, and blockers
- Adjust task priorities if needed



### As Needed — Emergency

**Duration:** Quick call (~5–15 minutes)

**Platform:** Zoom

**Purpose:** Resolve urgent issues or unblock tasks immediately.

**Format:**

- Focused discussion on a single problem
- Quick decision-making
