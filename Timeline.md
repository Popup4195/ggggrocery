# Timeline

* What is it? 

A schedule showing WHEN each phase happens and key deadlines. 

* Why use it?

Everyone knows the deadlines 

Prevents last-minute panic 

**Shows dependencies (X must finish before Y)**



## Requirements Document Timeline

Duration:  ~2 weeks

| Week   | Task                   | Description                                                  | Responsible      |
| ------ | ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------- |
| Week 4 | Initial Discussion     | Each team member presents their Problem Definition draft     | All team members |
| Week 4 | Consolidation          | Extract key points from individual drafts, identify core problems | All team members |
| Week 4 | Requirements Analysis  | Discuss and classify functional and non-functional requirements, assign priorities | All team members |
| Week 4 | Document Drafting      | Split the requirements document into 4 parts, each member writes their section | All team members |
| Week 5 | Review & Refinement    | One member checks and verifies document consistency          | PUYU, CAOZIJIAN  |
| Week 5 | Artefacts & Git Upload | One member compiles supporting artefacts and uploads everything to GitLab | PUYU             |
| Week 5 | Final Submission       | All team members confirm document, export PDF and submit the requirement document | All team members |
